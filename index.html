    Where we'll be

    The places

    What each stop is like in late February and early March, and where we're staying.

    1

    Ho Chi Minh City

    Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh

    Feb 13 – 16 · Feb 18 – 20

    Staying
    Amory Hotel in District 1, the walkable centre near Bến Thành Market (Feb 13 – 16). The second stay isn’t booked yet.
    Weather
    Hot, dry and sunny, highs around 32°C. About a week after Tết, so the city is back to normal.

    Mekong Delta

    Đồng bằng sông Cửu Long

    Feb 16 – 18

    Staying
    Guesthouses and a homestay along the river, on a small-group tour
    Weather
    Dry season, sunny and hot. Peak time for the floating markets.

    Hoi An

    Hội An

    Feb 20 – 24

    Staying
    KA Villa & Spa at An Bang Beach, a short bike ride from the Old Town
    Weather
    Warm and sunny, mid-20s°C, very little rain. Good beach weather.

    Ninh Binh

    Ninh Bình

    Feb 25 – Mar 1

    Staying
    Tam Coc Boutique Garden in Tam Cốc, among the rice paddies and limestone peaks
    Weather
    Cool and misty, around 16–22°C. The rice paddies are still green.

    Hanoi

    Hà Nội

    Mar 1 – 3 · Mar 5 – 7

    Staying
    Hanoi Saga Hotel (Mar 1 – 3) and Hanoi Hotel Royal (Mar 5 – 7), both in the Old Quarter near Hoàn Kiếm Lake
    Weather
    Cooler than the south, highs around 20–23°C, often grey with light drizzle.

    The itinerary

    The plan as it stands. Some days are loose and will change once we're there. Dates are local to wherever we are.

      Every day, in order

      1. Fri12Feb
        Day 1Travel dayToday

        Wheels up

        Leave Victoria in the morning, change in Vancouver and fly overnight to Seoul.

      2. Sat13Feb
        Day 2Ho Chi Minh CityToday

        Arrive in Saigon

        Change planes in Seoul and land in Ho Chi Minh City just before midnight.

      3. Sun14Feb
        Day 3Today

        First day in the city

        Shake off the jet lag around Bến Thành Market and the streets of District 1.

      4. Mon15Feb
        Day 4Today

        Saigon sights

        Jade Emperor Pagoda, the old Central Post Office and the Opera House, then a street-food crawl for bánh mì, phở and cơm tấm.

      5. Tue16Feb
        Day 5Mekong DeltaToday

        Into the Delta

        The small-group Mekong tour begins. Coconut plantations in Bến Tre, rowing a sampan and cycling along the waterways.

      6. Wed17Feb
        Day 6Today

        River life

        Cooking spring rolls and bánh xèo with a host family, then a lantern-lit evening in the village.

      7. Thu18Feb
        Day 7Today

        Floating market

        Cái Răng floating market at sunrise, a cycle past the brick-and-pottery village of Mang Thít, then back to Saigon.

      8. Fri19Feb
        Day 8Ho Chi Minh CityToday

        Back in Saigon

        A free day: Chợ Lớn (Chinatown), a rooftop view over the skyline or a sunset boat on the Saigon River.

      9. Sat20Feb
        Day 9Hoi AnToday

        North to the coast

        A short flight to Da Nang, then on to An Bang Beach outside Hoi An.

      10. Sun21Feb
        Day 10Today

        Lantern town

        Hoi An Old Town by bike: the Japanese Covered Bridge, the tailors, and the riverside lit up with lanterns at night.

      11. Mon22Feb
        Day 11Today

        Marble Mountains

        A day trip to the caves and pagodas of the Marble Mountains, or just the beach.

      12. Tue23Feb
        Day 12Today

        Beach day

        A slow day at An Bang Beach. Maybe a trip into Da Nang for the Dragon Bridge and the food scene.

      13. Wed24Feb
        Day 13Travel dayToday

        Overnight train

        An evening sleeper train from Da Nang north along the coast, about 14 hours.

      14. Thu25Feb
        Day 14Ninh BinhToday

        Arrive in Ninh Binh

        Off the train and into the countryside, sometimes called “Ha Long Bay on land”.

      15. Fri26Feb
        Day 15Today

        Tam Cốc by boat

        Rowed through the rice paddies and limestone caves of Tam Cốc or Tràng An.

      16. Sat27Feb
        Day 16Today

        Mua Cave

        Climbing the steps to the Mua Cave viewpoint, then Hoa Lư, the ancient capital.

      17. Sun28Feb
        Day 17Today

        By bike

        Cycling the back roads and visiting Bích Động pagoda.

      18. Mon1Mar
        Day 18HanoiToday

        On to Hanoi

      19. Tue2Mar
        Day 19Today

        Old Quarter

        Bún chả, egg coffee, Hoàn Kiếm Lake and Train Street.

      20. Wed3Mar
        Day 20Ha Long BayToday

      21. Thu4Mar
        Day 21Today

      22. Fri5Mar
        Day 22Today

      23. Sat6Mar
        Day 23HanoiToday

      24. Sun7Mar
        Day 24Travel dayToday
